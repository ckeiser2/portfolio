#### Story Outline
- Title: Reisender
- Setting: You awake after a mysterious dream to find that you are in outerspace on the spaceship the "Reisender", with an unreliable narrator in an AI named Bin navigating you through the ship. You become more suspicious of the bot throughout the story and uncover what happened to your crew mates.
- Main Character: The origins of the protagonist are a mystery. But they are a crew mate upon this vessel and to maintain health over a space exploration most humans were in cryo sleep with some left out to tend to the ship. Your cyro goes wrong and the player wakes up with amnesia like symptoms.
- Goal: The goal for the player is to figure out what has happened to their ship and crewmates. While also deducing what is being told to them is right or wrong from Bin.
- Conflict: The main conflict in the story is Bin. Listening to what it has to say or choosing your own route is the main conflict within the story. Or siding with Heute.
- Major Events: Leaving the medbay, choosing between traveling through an air lock or breaking the door, choosing to go into the cockpit before looking around, helping out Heute.
- Choices: Choosing to look around more provides more dialogue options that can give more context for decisions or choices down the line, choosing between traveling through a door or air lock, choosing between what to do first when arriving in the observatory are some major choices within the game.
- Endings: Ending 1: Choosing to go into the airlock when it has been alluded to that Bin does not have your best interest at heart. You explore the airlock until you eventually suffocate and get too hot. I hint at the idea that everytime you lose though, Bin revives you in a Cyro pod to repeat the cycle with you over again as a form of their entertainment. 

Ending 2: You go to the Cockpit before meeting Heute. This allows Bin to lock you in a room that it can remove all the oxygen out of. While you are fainting Bin gives you more context for it's actions and hints at this all being a loop for it.

Ending 3: You learn of Heute's back up drive that Bin didn't know of. Plugging her into the ship's head computer. This wipes Bin from the system and returns Hilde as the level headed ship computer. You are promoted to captain of Reisender to continue the ship's journey.



This is a simplified idea of Bin's design. Wanted it to be fairly simple similar to Space Odyssey or other works with Super Computer characters. Wanted to play with the idea that you can tell it has other models with other personalities stored within it with the eye colors.
