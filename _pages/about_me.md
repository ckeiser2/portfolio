#### **Colton Keiser**

Grew up in a rural area of central Illinois. Graduated from the **University of Illinois** with a **Bachelor’s in Information Sciences** and a Minor in Game Studies and Design in Spring 2024. Currently working as a Senior IT Auditor for a consulting firm while also involved in their internal application/tool development team as a **python backend developer** in St.Louis, MO.

My favorite game series is Nintendo's "Pikmin", a fun mixture of an RTS, puzzle, and adventure. With an interesting art style and engaging level design.

My career and educational goals would involve working in the game industry in a technical design role, being able to design mechanics, features and more while also contributing from a programming/scripting perspective. 

In my free time I enjoy learning new technical skills, gaming, reading and a mixture of weight and endurance training. I have run a couple 5k and 10ks this year. Additionally, pursuing a Unity junior developer and a Microsoft Power Platforms certification as well.
